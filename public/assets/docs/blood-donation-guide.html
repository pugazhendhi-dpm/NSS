Pre & Post Donation Care

Essential Steps for a Safe Experience
✅ BEFORE DONATION — DO THIS
✔Hydrate well — Drink 500 mL extra water 2 hrs before
✔Eat a light iron-rich meal — Include spinach, lentils, or eggs
✔Avoid fatty foods — Fat affects infection testing accuracy
✔Sleep 7–8 hours — Reduces dizziness risk
✔Carry valid photo ID — Aadhaar / PAN / Passport required
✔Wear loose clothing — Easy upper-arm access needed
✔Avoid alcohol 48 hrs prior — Affects blood quality
✔No strenuous exercise — On the day of donation
✔Disclose ALL medications — Full honest disclosure required
✅ AFTER DONATION — DO THIS
✔Rest 10–15 minutes — Stay in the observation area
✔Press & keep plaster on — For at least 3–4 hours
✔Drink extra fluids — Juice, water, coconut water
✔Eat snacks provided — Biscuit and juice at donation camp
✔Avoid driving 30 min — Possible light-headedness
✔No heavy lifting 24 hrs — Rest arm used for donation
✔Avoid alcohol 24 hrs — Post-donation dehydration risk
✔Exercise next day only — Light activity okay after 4 hrs
✔Iron-rich diet 7 days — Red meat, spinach, legumes

Indian Regulatory Framework

Legal & Official Guidelines — NBTC, CDSCO, Drugs & Cosmetics Act
Regulation / BodyKey Provision
Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940 (Schedule F, Part XII-B)Governs licensing, operation & quality standards of all blood banks in India
National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC)Apex body under MoHFW; sets all national standards & policies for blood transfusion
State Blood Transfusion Councils (SBTCs)Implement NBTC policies; oversee state-level blood bank licensing
Voluntary Blood Donation PolicyAll paid/commercial blood donation is BANNED in India since 1998
CDSCO (Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation)Licenses blood banks; conducts compliance inspections
NACO (National AIDS Control Organisation)Mandates HIV screening on every single donated unit
Blood Bank LicensingEvery blood bank must hold a valid licence from State Drug Controller
Informed ConsentDonor must sign a voluntary consent form before donation proceeds
Medical Officer CertificationA registered MO must certify donor fitness before blood is drawn
Donor Deferral RegisterAll deferred donors must be recorded; register reviewed quarterly
Blood Component PreparationMandatory separation into RBCs, plasma, platelets where feasible
Labelling StandardsEvery bag: blood group, Rh, volume, date, expiry, screening status
Cold Chain RequirementsWhole blood: 2–6°C | Platelets: 20–24°C agitated | FFP: ≤ −25°C
Cross-Matching MandateCompulsory ABO + Rh cross-match before any transfusion
Haemovigilance Programme (HvPI)All adverse transfusion reactions must be reported to CDSCO/NBTC
Lookback / TraceabilityEach donation traceable from donor to recipient for up to 30 years
